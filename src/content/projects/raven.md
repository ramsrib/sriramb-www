Odin's ravens flew out each day and came back to whisper reports of everything they'd
seen.

Run several agents at once and the bottleneck stops being the agents — it's noticing
which one has stopped and is waiting on you. Raven watches every live Claude Code
session on the machine and shows you exactly that: what's active, what's waiting, what's
parked, and what each one is burning.

Interactive TUI only. Its natural habitat is a dedicated tab on a tall monitor, off to
the side, telling you where to look next.
