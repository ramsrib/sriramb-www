Warp got the terminal right — blocks, modern input, a genuinely good rendering
engine — and then wrapped it in an account system, a cloud backend, and an AI layer I
didn't want in the one tool I live in all day.

Tarp is that terminal with those layers removed. Not AI-native. Not cloud-first. No
account to sign into, and no telemetry leaving the machine. What's left is the part
that was actually good.

It's a fork, so it inherits Warp's foundations and stays close to them. The work is
mostly subtraction.
