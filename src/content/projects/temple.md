Once you run more than a couple of coding agents, the sessions become the thing you
lose. They live in terminal tabs, they're named after nothing, and finding the one you
were in twenty minutes ago means squinting at a tab bar.

Temple organizes sessions by project and keeps them all a click away. You switch
between them without losing your place, search every session you've run, and pick up
where you left off — it reopens the sessions you had open. Full terminal, not a
transcript viewer, so the agent behaves exactly as it does anywhere else.

The part I use most: seeing at a glance which agents are working and which are waiting
on me.
