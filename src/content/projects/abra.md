*abracadabra* — from the Aramaic **avra kehdabra**, "I create as I speak."

Hold Fn, say the thing, release. Clean text appears wherever your cursor already is —
an editor, a terminal, an agent prompt, a text field in some app that has never heard
of dictation.

Everything runs on-device. Nothing you say leaves your Mac, which is the whole reason
I stopped using the alternatives.

Talking to coding agents is what pushed me to build it: prompts are long, and typing
them is slower than saying them.
